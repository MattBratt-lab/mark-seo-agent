## Strategic Overview

A garage door repair business competes in a **high-intent, mobile-first, emergency-driven** local market. The customer decision cycle is often under 10 minutes — someone's door is stuck, they search on their phone, and they call the first result that looks trustworthy.

**Winning in this market requires:**
1. **Map Pack visibility** — top 3 in Google Maps for "[service] [city]"
2. **Organic page 1** — for service + city combinations
3. **Trust signals** — reviews, photos, credentials, E-E-A-T
4. **Speed** — fast site, fast phone answer, fast service dispatch

**Current position:** 1 indexable page, ~288 words, no GBP signals, blocked from AI search, broken blog.  
**Target position:** 30+ pages, 4.5+ stars (25 reviews), top-3 Map Pack in 5 cities, 300+ organic sessions/month.

## E-E-A-T Strategy (Google Quality Rater Guidelines — Sept 2025)

For a garage door repair business, E-E-A-T signals that actually move rankings:

### Experience
- **Photos of real jobs** — before/after of spring replacements, installations, cable repairs
- **Years in business** stated clearly with proof (incorporated date, etc.)
- **Number of jobs completed** (e.g., "5,000+ doors repaired")
- **Video content** showing technician at work

### Expertise
- **Technician bios** with certifications (LiftMaster Authorized Dealer, DASMA)
- **Florida contractor license number** displayed
- **Technical content** explaining *why* springs fail, how openers work
- **Specific brand knowledge** (Clopay, Wayne Dalton, Chamberlain, LiftMaster)

### Authoritativeness
- **Industry citations** — get mentioned on Angi, HomeAdvisor, BBB
- **"Best of" lists** — target "best garage door company in Palm Beach County" articles
- **Local media** — any local news or community mention
- **Review quantity** — 25+ verified Google reviews signals authority

### Trustworthiness
- **Insurance/bonding stated**
- **License number visible**
- **Real phone number + email** (both present — good)
- **Physical service area map** (even SABs benefit)
- **No broken functionality** (fix the blog and debug messages)
- **HTTPS + security headers**

## GBP Strategy (2026 Guidelines)

Per the template's updated GBP guidelines:

1. **Video verification** — prepare a short video walkthrough of service vehicle / tools
2. **No WhatsApp integration yet** — use phone as primary CTA (already doing this)
3. **Q&A replaced by AI answers** — ensure GBP description + website FAQ are comprehensive (AI uses these)
4. **Business hours = top-5 ranking factor** (2026 Whitespark report) — keep accurate, consider extending hours
5. **Review Stories format** — encourage detailed reviews with photos; they appear in swipeable Stories on Maps mobile
6. **SAB service area** — cannot list "Florida" or "Palm Beach County"; must list specific cities

**GBP Categories (Recommended):**
- Primary: `Garage door supplier`
- Secondary: `Garage door installation service`, `Door repair service`, `Home improvement service`

## AI Search (GEO) Strategy

AI Overviews appear for only ~0.14% of local keywords — local SEO faces less AI disruption than other verticals. However, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude are increasingly used for local recommendations like "who should I call for garage door repair in Boca Raton?"

**Priority actions:**
1. Allow Google-Extended + GPTBot in robots.txt
2. Add `llms.txt` with service catalog and contact info
3. Get onto "best garage door repair" curated lists (ranked #1 AI visibility factor)
4. Complete LocalBusiness schema with `geo`, `openingHours`, `priceRange`, `areaServed`
5. Add FAQ content structured for AI extraction
