## Our Services

Professional garage door solutions with fast response times. Get a free quote for any service below.

### [Garage Door Installation](https://www.dandbgaragedoors.com/services/garage-door-installation/)

At D and B Garage Doors, we provide exceptional garage door installation services that feature a variety of styles and designs. Our team of experts is ready to assist you in selecting the ideal option for your home and will ensure it is installed to the highest standard. We also offer emergency services and reliable garage door repair to address any urgent needs you may have.

[Learn more](https://www.dandbgaragedoors.com/services/garage-door-installation/)

### [Emergency Garage Door Repair](https://www.dandbgaragedoors.com/services/emergency-garage-door-repair/)

If your garage door is not functioning properly, our technicians can quickly and efficiently provide garage door repair services. We offer emergency garage door services to diagnose and fix issues you may have. We use high-quality parts so your garage door operates smoothly for years. If you're considering a new setup, we also specialize in garage door installation.

[Learn more](https://www.dandbgaragedoors.com/services/emergency-garage-door-repair/)

### [Garage Door Opener Repair](https://www.dandbgaragedoors.com/services/garage-door-opener-repair/)

If your opener won't respond, reverses unexpectedly, or makes grinding noises, we'll diagnose sensors, settings, and drive components to restore smooth, reliable operation. We focus on safe fixes that prevent repeat failures.

[Learn more](https://www.dandbgaragedoors.com/services/garage-door-opener-repair/)

### [Garage Door Spring Repair](https://www.dandbgaragedoors.com/services/garage-door-spring-repair/)

Broken or worn springs can make your door dangerously heavy and strain the opener. We replace springs with the correct sizing and balance so the door lifts smoothly and safely.

[Learn more](https://www.dandbgaragedoors.com/services/garage-door-spring-repair/)

### [Garage Door Cable Repair](https://www.dandbgaragedoors.com/services/garage-door-cable-repair/)

Frayed or snapped cables can cause uneven lifting and off-track issues. We replace lift cables and reset the system so your door tracks level and operates safely.

[Learn more](https://www.dandbgaragedoors.com/services/garage-door-cable-repair/)

### [Garage Door Tune-Up](https://www.dandbgaragedoors.com/services/garage-door-tune-up/)

Prevent breakdowns and reduce noise with a full inspection, lubrication, adjustments, and safety checks. A tune-up helps your door run smoother and protects your opener over time.

[Learn more](https://www.dandbgaragedoors.com/services/garage-door-tune-up/)
